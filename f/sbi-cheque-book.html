Cheque book request (SBI) (चेक बुक मँगाना (एसबीआई))

SBI Cheque Book Request (New Cheque Book)

How to ask State Bank of India for a new cheque book. SBI's policy says a cheque book can be requested at the branch, through Internet Banking (OnlineSBI), YONO, SBI Quick (SMS) and the Contact Centre; SBI's ATM page also offers a Cheque Book Request. SBI issues Multi City (CTS-2010) cheques, payable at par at all its branches. The cheque book is sent by post to your last recorded address (or the address given while requesting), and SBI sends an SMS when the request is recorded, when it is dispatched (with a tracking number from India Post), and when it is delivered. So before you ask, make sure your address and mobile number are correct in the bank's records. A savings account gets its first 10 cheque leaves free in a financial year (charges as on 30.09.2025). If a cheque or the whole cheque book is lost, ask SBI at once to stop payment of those cheques.

What to write in each box

  1. Before you ask: who can get a cheque book: Cheque account, registered mobile number, KYC complete, correct address
    माँगने से पहले: चेक बुक किसे मिलती है: चेक वाला खाता, रजिस्टर्ड मोबाइल, पूरी KYC, सही पता
  2. Number of leaves, and Bearer or Order cheques: Savings account: 10 or 25 leaves; bearer by default
    कितने पन्ने, और बेयरर या ऑर्डर चेक: बचत खाता: 10 या 25 पन्ने; आम तौर पर बेयरर चेक
  3. At the branch (including emergency cheque book): Ask branch staff for request slip; give account number, sign
    ब्रांच में (इमरजेंसी चेक बुक समेत): स्टाफ़ से रिक्वेस्ट स्लिप लें; खाता नंबर लिखें, साइन करें
  4. Internet Banking (OnlineSBI): Request & Enquiries > Cheque Book Services > Cheque book request
    इंटरनेट बैंकिंग (OnlineSBI): Request & Enquiries > Cheque Book Services > Cheque book request
  5. YONO app: Manage Cheque Book > Request for a new Cheque Book
    YONO ऐप: Manage Cheque Book > नई चेक बुक की रिक्वेस्ट
  6. SBI ATM (with your SBI debit card): At an SBI ATM choose Cheque Book Request
    SBI ATM (अपने SBI डेबिट कार्ड से): SBI ATM में Cheque Book Request चुनें
  7. SMS (SBI Quick): CHQREQ: From registered mobile, SMS CHQREQ to 917208933145, then send consent
    SMS (SBI Quick): CHQREQ: रजिस्टर्ड मोबाइल से CHQREQ भेजें 917208933145 पर, फिर सहमति SMS
  8. Phone: SBI Contact Centre 1800 1234: Call 1800 1234, choose language, press 2, then 4
    फ़ोन: SBI कॉन्टैक्ट सेंटर 1800 1234: 1800 1234 पर कॉल, भाषा चुनें, 2 दबाएँ, फिर 4
  9. Charges (savings account, as on 30.09.2025): First 10 leaves free each year; then ₹40/₹75 + GST
    चार्ज (बचत खाता, 30.09.2025 तक): हर साल पहले 10 पन्ने मुफ़्त; फिर ₹40/₹75 + GST
  10. Delivery and tracking: Posted to registered address; track via SMS, YONO or 1800 1234
    डिलीवरी और ट्रैकिंग: रजिस्टर्ड पते पर डाक से; SMS, YONO या 1800 1234 से ट्रैक करें
  11. Lost cheque or cheque book: stop payment: Stop payment at once: branch, YONO, internet banking, ATM, 1800 1234
    चेक या चेक बुक खो जाए: भुगतान रुकवाएँ (stop payment): तुरंत stop payment: ब्रांच में लिखकर, YONO, इंटरनेट बैंकिंग, ATM, 1800 1234
