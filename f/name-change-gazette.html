Name change (Gazette) (नाम बदलना (गज़ट))

Change of Name in the Gazette of India (Adult)

If you are 18 or older and have changed your name (or added or removed a surname), you can get it published in the Gazette of India, Part IV, which carries notices by private individuals. The Department of Publication, Civil Lines, Delhi-110054 handles it. Steps: (1) Advertise the change in one daily local leading newspaper, giving your father's or husband's name and residential address; keep the original newspaper. (2) Type the proforma (specimen) in duplicate on plain paper, sign it in your OLD name with two witnesses. (3) Sign the undertaking. (4) Put the proforma matter (without the witness part, with your old name typed in place of the signature) on a CD in MS Word, and sign a CD certificate. (5) Pay the fee on bharatkosh.gov.in and print the receipt. (6) Send everything with a request letter, two self-attested passport size photos and a self-attested photo ID to the Controller of Publications, by hand or by post/courier. The guidelines (updated 30.10.2026) do not mention any online application; agents and advocates are not allowed. Documents must not be older than one year and are never returned. The printed Gazette stopped on 1 October 2015; after publication you download your copy from egazette.gov.in (Search Gazette, Weekly Gazette, Part IV), and it needs no further certification. Some matters, such as correction of date of birth, are not published in Part IV. Children (minors) have separate guidelines. The eGazette portal also has a separate "State Gazettes" section; this guide is only for the Gazette of India.

What to write in each box

  1. Proforma: "I hitherto known as (Old Name)": Your current (old) name, same as your ID proof
    प्रोफ़ॉर्मा: "I hitherto known as (पुराना नाम)": अपना अभी वाला (पुराना) नाम, आईडी प्रूफ़ जैसा
  2. Proforma: "Son/daughter/Wife of (Father/Husband's name)": Keep son/daughter/wife that fits; father's or husband's name
    प्रोफ़ॉर्मा: "Son/daughter/Wife of (पिता/पति का नाम)": बेटा/बेटी/पत्नी में से सही रखें; पिता या पति का नाम
  3. Proforma: "employed as (Post) in the (Office/Company's name)": Your job post and employer; not working? ask the Department
    प्रोफ़ॉर्मा: "employed as (पद) in the (दफ़्तर/कंपनी का नाम)": आपका पद और कंपनी/दफ़्तर; नौकरी नहीं? विभाग से पूछें
  4. Proforma: "residing at (residential address)": Your full residential address with PIN code
    प्रोफ़ॉर्मा: "residing at (घर का पता)": पिन कोड के साथ घर का पूरा पता
  5. Proforma: "shall hereafter be known as (New Name)": Your new name, exactly as in the newspaper advertisement
    प्रोफ़ॉर्मा: "shall hereafter be known as (नया नाम)": नया नाम, बिल्कुल अख़बार के विज्ञापन जैसा
  6. Proforma: "Type here your Old Name" and "Signature (in existing Old Name)": Type old name; sign in your OLD name
    प्रोफ़ॉर्मा: "Type here your Old Name" और "Signature (पुराने नाम में)": पुराना नाम टाइप करें; पुराने नाम से साइन करें
  7. Proforma: Witness No. 1 and Witness No. 2 (Full Name, Signature, Address, Mobile): Two witnesses: full name, signature, address, mobile
    प्रोफ़ॉर्मा: गवाह नंबर 1 और गवाह नंबर 2 (पूरा नाम, साइन, पता, मोबाइल): दो गवाह: पूरा नाम, साइन, पता, मोबाइल
  8. Undertaking: "I ... S/o, W/o, D/o ... R/o ... Marital status": Name, father's/husband's name, address, marital status
    अंडरटेकिंग: "मैं ... S/o, W/o, D/o ... R/o ... वैवाहिक स्थिति": नाम, पिता/पति का नाम, पता, वैवाहिक स्थिति
  9. Undertaking: points 1 to 4, Signature, Name, Father's/Spouse Name, Present Address, Mobile No., Email id: Read points 1–4, then sign and fill your details
    अंडरटेकिंग: बिंदु 1 से 4, साइन, नाम, पिता/पति-पत्नी का नाम, वर्तमान पता, मोबाइल नंबर, ईमेल: बिंदु 1–4 पढ़ें, फिर साइन करें और अपनी जानकारी भरें
  10. Request letter: Subject and "from (Old name) to (New name)": Strike what doesn't apply; write old and new name
    रिक्वेस्ट लेटर: विषय और "from (पुराना नाम) to (नया नाम)": जो लागू न हो उसे हटाएँ; पुराना और नया नाम लिखें
  11. Request letter: Signature, Name, Full correspondence address with Pin Code, Mobile No., Email ID: Sign; name, postal address with PIN, mobile, email
    रिक्वेस्ट लेटर: साइन, नाम, पिन कोड के साथ पत्राचार का पूरा पता, मोबाइल नंबर, ईमेल: साइन; नाम, पिन कोड वाला पता, मोबाइल, ईमेल
  12. CD certificate: "I (old Name) Son/daughter/wife of ... Address ..." and Signature: Old name, father's/husband's name, address; sign in old name
    CD सर्टिफ़िकेट: "मैं (पुराना नाम) बेटा/बेटी/पत्नी ... पता ..." और साइन: पुराना नाम, पिता/पति का नाम, पता; पुराने नाम से साइन
